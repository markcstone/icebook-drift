Icebook on Salt Creek

On the afternoon of 6 November 2026 we put an instrumented block of ice into Salt Creek in Lincoln, Nebraska, and let the current take it. This page follows the 55 minute drift through the block's own GPS track and the photographs it took along the way.

Salt Creek, Lincoln NE

All photographs. Click any to enlarge.

Track: Icebook onboard GPS, 393 fixes at about 8 second intervals, 6 November 2026, 16:31 to 17:26 Central. Photos from the Icebook camera are placed on the track by capture time; phone photos carry their own position. Photos were tone adjusted for consistency between the two cameras; nothing was added or removed. Imagery: Esri World Imagery (Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics, and the GIS User Community). Map: MapLibre GL JS.
